# Risks

| ID    | Risk                                                                                         | Level  | Mitigation                                                                                                                  | Residual / verification                                          |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R-001 | Stock photos could be mistaken for real SAUCARA work.                                        | medium | Use local licensed files, descriptive alt text, asset documentation, and visible demo-photography disclosure.               | Verified; owner photography is still required before launch.                     |
| R-002 | A numberless WhatsApp link proves message preparation but does not reach a business account. | medium | Centralize recipient configuration, label demo behavior, keep message useful, and document production requirement.          | Verified for demo; owner must supply an approved number before launch.            |
| R-003 | Long one-page content can bury conversion on mobile.                                         | medium | Compact copy, contextual CTAs, strong anchors, no obstructive floating bar.                                                 | Verified at tested widths; real visitor analytics are intentionally unavailable. |
| R-004 | Gold-on-cream or fine serif typography can fail contrast/readability.                        | high   | Use gold only on deep green or decorative rules; use darker gold ink on cream; maintain 16px+ body and test contrast/zoom.  | Automated axe and independent manual contrast review passed.                     |
| R-005 | Image/font weight can degrade LCP and layout stability.                                      | medium | Local WOFF2, responsive Next Image, intrinsic dimensions, preload only hero, lazy-load below fold, compress assets.         | Lab review passed; production field LCP/INP remains unmeasured.                  |
| R-006 | Form success language could imply a sent/confirmed order.                                    | high   | Define success as “message prepared,” expose a separate outbound link, and test forbidden wording.                          | Unit/component/E2E and independent copy review passed.                           |
| R-007 | Demo policies could be read as real commercial/legal terms.                                  | medium | Label hours, testimonials, policies, and contact details fictional; set `noindex`; avoid guarantees and exact legal claims. | Independent review passed; replace all demo operations before launch.            |
| R-008 | External WhatsApp/Pexels/font availability could make build/runtime unstable.                | low    | Download assets/fonts locally; no runtime third-party requests except user-initiated WhatsApp navigation.                   | Runtime audit passed; WhatsApp availability remains external.                    |
| R-009 | Browser-specific behavior could differ outside the tested Chromium engine.                    | medium | Use semantic/native controls, standards-based CSS, keyboard tests, reduced motion, and progressive enhancement.             | Safari, Firefox, physical devices, and a real screen reader remain untested.      |
